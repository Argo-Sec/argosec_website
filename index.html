Sector specific use cases

Financial

Due to handling a vast amount of confidential data and sensitive transactions, ArgoSec provides highly sophisticated data protection solutions, including advanced encryption, AI-based fraud prevention systems, and constant monitoring of suspicious activity. We also offer penetration testing services to identify vulnerabilities in online banking applications and advice on compliance with specific financial regulations.

Technology and Telecommunications

We focus on protecting the technological infrastructure that supports global connectivity. ArgoSec implements advanced threat detection solutions, behavioral analysis, and multi-factor authentication to ensure networks and systems are safeguarded against cyber attacks. Additionally, our emphasis is on protecting customer's sensitive data and preventing cyber espionage through encryption and risk analysis solutions.

Transportation and Manufacturing

We protect critical operations and the supply chain. ArgoSec provides industrial control system (ICS) security solutions, such as specialized firewalls and network segmentation. We also conduct penetration testing on transportation and manufacturing systems to identify potential entry points for attackers. Furthermore, we offer education and training services for employees on best security practices.

Public Sector

It's vital to protect citizens' sensitive information and critical infrastructure. ArgoSec assists in implementing data security solutions, such as encryption and authentication, in record management systems and databases. We also provide continuous threat monitoring services to quickly detect and respond to potential security incidents, and advise on creating robust security policies.

Wholesale

We protect data for businesses engaged in wholesale purchasing and ensure reliable e-commerce platforms. ArgoSec implements data security solutions, conducts security audits on online platforms, and offers penetration testing services on applications and systems to ensure data protection.

Energy and Utilities

We implement intrusion detection systems in electrical grids and renewable energy infrastructures, along with providing data security solutions and industrial control systems. We also offer risk assessment and audit services to identify potential weaknesses in the infrastructure.
